Pong Reimagined, playable.

A one-button arcade game from a 2023 game jam, rebuilt for the browser. Keep the ball alive, bounce off the green goals, and dodge the voids.

Space / click / tap: flap P pause M mute

On a phone? Turn it sideways for a bigger view.

Your best score is saved in this browser. The game pauses automatically when you switch tabs.

How to play

  • Gravity pulls the ball down. Flap to stay in the air.
  • Hit the green goal on the far wall to score and bounce back.
  • Red walls and the spinning green voids destroy you.
  • At 15 and 40 points the ball speeds up and more voids appear.

What's new in the remake

  • Runs in any browser, including phones, with no install
  • Menu, countdown, level banners, and a saved best score
  • Fairer voids that flash before they become deadly and never spawn in your path
  • Particles, trails, and glowing goals; screen shake respects reduced-motion settings
  • Sound effects generated in code, so no audio files are needed

The original

Pong Reimagined started as a Python and Pygame game made by Team Xyke for a game jam in October 2023. I was the team's project manager. The hand-drawn backgrounds and sprites are the team's own artwork.

The jam version and this remake are both on GitHub.
